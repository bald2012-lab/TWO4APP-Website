Privacy policy — Vergissmeinnicht

App-specific privacy information for Vergissmeinnicht on Android, based on version 1.20 TEST.

Last updated: 2 Nov 2026 · Draft

1. Controller and contact

Provider: [FULL LEGAL NAME], TWO4APP, [POSTAL ADDRESS], [PRIVACY EMAIL]. This information must be completed before publication.

2. What information is processed?

The app locally processes the reminders you create: titles, dates, times, recurrence rules, notes, checklists, selected icons and tones, and display/notification preferences. Selecting a custom audio file can create a local copy of the chosen sound.

3. On-device storage and no automatic transmission

In the currently reviewed Android version, reminders and settings reside in the app’s private storage on your device. Version 1.20 TEST has no Android INTERNET permission and no integrated advertising or analytics SDK. Automatic upload of reminders to TWO4APP, TWO4APP cloud synchronization and a TWO4APP account system are not implemented.

4. Android permissions and notifications

The app uses Android permissions to display notifications, schedule precise alarm times and restore reminders after device restart. Android system services deliver the notifications. Depending on device settings, app identity, reminder names and note text may appear on the lock screen. The current app marks notifications as publicly visible on the lock screen.

5. Custom audio and system access

Selected custom notification sounds may be copied to local storage. A restricted read-only Android content provider lets the system retrieve the corresponding audio files for notifications. It does not expose reminder note files.

6. Backup and import

You can export and import a ZIP backup of reminders and selected audio files. The exported ZIP is not encrypted by the app. Any external storage you choose, including a cloud provider, is subject to that provider’s privacy and security arrangements.

7. Retention and deletion

Reminders remain on the device until deleted or the app’s private data is removed. Uninstalling the app normally causes Android to erase that private storage. Backups you exported remain until separately deleted. The app currently makes no automatic TWO4APP cloud backup.

8. Trial and future purchases

Version 1.20 TEST has no active Google Play purchase flow and no enabled trial expiration enforcement. This policy must be updated before introducing Google Play Billing or a server-based license verification system to cover actual payment, identification and license data.

9. Your rights and requests

Where the provider processes personal data, relevant GDPR data subject rights apply. For data held only on your device and inaccessible to TWO4APP, you can use the app’s edit, export and deletion controls or Android’s app data controls. A privacy contact email will be provided before launch.

Important: This policy describes the reviewed 1.20 TEST codebase. Before commercial release, the app, libraries, permissions and Google Play payment flow must be audited again.
