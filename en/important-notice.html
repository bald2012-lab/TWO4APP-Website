Important notice

Reliability of Android notifications

Last updated: 2 Nov 2026 · Draft

No guarantee of notification delivery

Vergissmeinnicht helps manage personal reminders, but notifications depend on Android, permissions, battery optimization and device-specific background restrictions. Alerts can be delayed, suppressed or delivered without sound.

Critical reminders

Do not rely on the app as your only reminder system for medically important, safety-critical or other high-consequence events. Always use an independent backup reminder.

Review device settings

Check “Permissions & reliability” in the app. Make sure notifications, exact alarms where necessary, and background operation are permitted.
