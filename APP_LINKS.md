`https://two4app.de/privacy/vergissmeinnicht.html`

No APK changes are necessary to update legal copy at the fixed link later. Switching a hard-coded URL from the temporary GitHub Pages address to the branded domain *would* require an APK update; therefore prefer the domain only after DNS and HTTPS are configured.

## Publication blockers
- The current German and English Impressum pages contain intentionally fictitious contact/address details for layout and navigation testing. This is NOT a valid Impressum.
- Privacy/controller information and support email also remain placeholders.
- Legal pages must be finalized for paid public use and Google Play submission.
- GitHub Pages must be enabled (Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main / root); DNS and custom domain setup are separate.
